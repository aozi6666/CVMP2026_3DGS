\section{RELATED WORK}
\label{sec:related}

Neural Radiance Fields (NeRF) represent scenes as implicit radiance fields and achieve high-quality novel view synthesis through differentiable rendering~\cite{Mildenhall21_NeRF}. Under sparse views, however, insufficient observations cause severe geometric ambiguity. Depth-guided approaches further improve structural consistency~\cite{Wang23_SparseNeRF,Deng22_DSNeRF}, while other methods constrain unobserved regions~\cite{Niemeyer22_RegNeRF}, jointly refine noisy poses~\cite{Truong23_SPARF}, or leverage diffusion priors~\cite{Wu24_ReconFusion}. 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) introduces an explicit scene representation based on optimizable Gaussian primitives and enables efficient training and real-time rendering through differentiable rasterization~\cite{Kerbl23_3DGS}. Beyond the original formulation, structured and anti-aliased Gaussian representations improve robustness and multi-scale rendering~\cite{Lu24_ScaffoldGS,Yu24_MipSplatting}, while surface-aligned variants target more accurate geometry recovery~\cite{Guedon24_SuGaR,Huang24_2DGS}. \rev{Depth-based approaches introduce additional geometric supervision}~\cite{Li24_DNGaussian}, \rev{while densification-based methods improve Gaussian coverage}~\cite{Zhu24_FSGS,Bao25_LoopSparseGS}. \rev{Other strategies include progressive geometric propagation}~\cite{Cheng24_GaussianPro}, \rev{gradient-aware density control}~\cite{Zhou26_GDAGS}, \rev{and epipolar depth priors}~\cite{Zheng25_NexusGS}. Others explore alternating densification under sparse inputs~\cite{Patle25_ADGS}, score distillation~\cite{Xiong24_SparseGS}, co-regularization~\cite{Zhang24_CoRGS}, and Gaussian dropout~\cite{Park25_DropGaussian,Fang26_DropAnSH}. Pose-free and feed-forward sparse Gaussian reconstruction has also been studied~\cite{Wang24_DUSt3R,Fan24_InstantSplat,Charatan24_pixelSplat,Chen24_MVSplat}.

Classical multi-view stereo remains a strong geometric backbone~\cite{Cao24_MVSFormerPP}. Monocular depth estimators provide useful cues for underconstrained views~\cite{Yang24_DepthAnythingV2}, and recent monocular geometry models further recover metric structure from a single image~\cite{Wang24_MoGe}. \rev{Although depth supervision, Gaussian densification, and training-time regularization have improved sparse-view reconstruction, limited-view observations can still result in incomplete initial geometry and overfitting during optimization. To address these challenges, SynGS combines explicit visual-hull constraints with a VGGT-based multi-view depth prior}~\cite{Wang25_VGGT} \rev{to augment the initial Gaussian geometry before optimization, and incorporates Dynamic Visibility Regularization during training to alleviate sparse-view overfitting.}
