\section{CONCLUSION}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We present SynGS for sparse-view 3DGS, combining \textbf{Visual Hull Initialization}, \textbf{VGGT / Depth-Guided Densification}, and \textbf{Dynamic Visibility Regularization} for more reliable initialization, geometry completion, and reduced sparse-view overfitting. On Mip-NeRF 360 and LLFF 360, SynGS improves novel-view quality under sparse inputs; densification adds memory and compute cost, and more efficient densification without losing quality is left for future work.
