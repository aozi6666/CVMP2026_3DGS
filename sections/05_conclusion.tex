\section{CONCLUSION}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We present SynGS for sparse-view 3DGS, combining \textbf{Visual Hull Initialization}, \textbf{VGGT-Guided Densification}, and \textbf{Dynamic Visibility Regularization} for more reliable initialization, geometry completion, and reduced sparse-view overfitting.
\rev{SynGS shows more pronounced improvements under highly sparse-view conditions, particularly in PSNR and SSIM with four or six input views. With nine input views, SynGS maintains higher PSNR and SSIM than the compared methods on Mip-NeRF 360, while the LLFF results remain comparable to those of other evaluated methods. However, the additional geometry introduced during initialization incurs memory and computational overhead. Future work will focus on developing more efficient geometry augmentation strategies to reduce these costs while preserving reconstruction quality.}
