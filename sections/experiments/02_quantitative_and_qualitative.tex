\textbf{Mip-NeRF 360 and LLFF.}
Table~\ref{tab:mip360}
\rev{reports the results on Mip-NeRF 360 under the 4-, 6-, and 9-view settings. SynGS achieves the highest PSNR and SSIM in all three settings, with the largest PSNR gain in the 4-view case: 25.91~dB, 3.56~dB above D\textsuperscript{2}GS}
~\cite{Song26_D2GS}. \rev{However, D\textsuperscript{2}GS obtains lower LPIPS values across the three settings, indicating an advantage in perceptual similarity.}
Table~\ref{tab:llff}
\rev{shows a similar trend. With four views, SynGS leads in PSNR, SSIM, and LPIPS; under six views it obtains the highest PSNR and SSIM with LPIPS close to the best result. As the number of input views increases to nine, the differences among the leading methods become smaller: D\textsuperscript{2}GS}
~\cite{Song26_D2GS}
\rev{gives slightly higher PSNR and lower LPIPS, while CoR-GS}
~\cite{Zhang24_CoRGS}
\rev{achieves slightly higher SSIM. Overall, SynGS shows more pronounced improvements in the four- and six-view settings, suggesting that the proposed framework is particularly beneficial when the geometric information available from the input views is severely limited.}
